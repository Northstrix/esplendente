Linguistic Complexity Rules for Definitions & Breakdowns
1. Definition Complexity Scale

LEVEL 1 (Simple): Single base-form words → Pure meaning + 1 example
"Cierto" → "Describes certainty or confidence. Ex: \"Estoy cierto\"."

LEVEL 2 (Moderate): Forms/derivatives → Form + meaning + context
"Curvo" → "Singular masculine form of \"curvo\". Describes something bent or arched."

LEVEL 3 (Complex): Multi-word/phrases → Full breakdown | meaning
"Por favor" → "\"Por\" | \"favor\". A polite expression used to make a request."

2. Breakdown Rules (ONLY when triggered)

APPLY breakdown (| separator) WHEN:

1. 2+ WORDS: "Por favor" → "\"Por\" | \"favor\""
2. NON-BASE VERB FORM: "Hablaste" → "\"Hablaste\" (past completed singular informal form of \"hablar\")"
3. NON-BASE NOUN: "Follaje" → "\"Follaje\" (collective noun related to \"hoja\")"
4. PRONOUN COMBOS: "Dímelo" → "\"Di\" | \"me\" | \"lo\""

NO breakdown for:

- Base infinitive: "Recordar" → No "|"
- Base singular masculine noun: "Río" → No "|"
- Single simple nouns: "Bestia" → No "|"

3. Complexity by Word Type

Single base verb: No breakdown.
Conjugated verb: Always include form + meaning.
2+ word phrase: Always break down every word.
Adjective not in masculine singular: Include form origin.
Diminutives or derived nouns: Include origin when useful.

4. Definition Length Rule

Base form → 1 sentence
Derived form → 1 sentence for form + 1 sentence for meaning
Multi-word → Breakdown | 1 descriptive sentence
